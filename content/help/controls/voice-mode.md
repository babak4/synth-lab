**What it does.** Poly plays chords. Mono plays one note at a time. Legato is mono too, but joined notes glide without restarting the envelopes.

**Range.** Poly, mono or legato.

**Tip.** Use mono or legato for bass and lead lines. They sound more focused, like a single instrument.
