**What it does.** Sets how much of each echo is fed back in, so that it repeats again. This is [feedback](term:feedback).

**Range.** 0% to 90%. It is capped at 90% so that the echoes always die away.

**Tip.** A low setting gives one or two repeats. A high setting gives a long trail of echoes that fade slowly.
