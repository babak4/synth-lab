**What it does.** Sets how long the volume takes to rise to its peak after you press a key. This is the [attack](term:attack) stage.

**Range.** 1 ms to 5 s. It starts at 5 ms.

**Tip.** Short attacks sound struck or plucked. Attacks of half a second or more fade in, like a bowed string or a pad.
