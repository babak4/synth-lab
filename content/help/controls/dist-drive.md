**What it does.** Sets how hard the signal is pushed into the [distortion](term:distortion). A harder push squashes the peaks of the wave and adds harmonics.

**Range.** 0 to 36 dB. It starts at 12 dB.

**Tip.** Low drive adds warmth. High drive gives a fuzzy, aggressive edge. Lower the tone control to tame any fizz.
