**What it does.** Chooses what the filter does: [low-pass](term:low-pass) keeps the lows, [high-pass](term:high-pass) keeps the highs, [band-pass](term:band-pass) keeps a band, and a [notch](term:notch) removes a band.

**Range.** Four types, all with a gentle [slope](term:slope) of 12 dB per octave.

**Tip.** Low-pass is the classic synth sound. Try band-pass with some resonance for a nasal, wah-like tone.
