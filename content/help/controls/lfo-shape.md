**What it does.** Chooses the pattern the [LFO](term:lfo) follows: smooth sine or triangle, a rising sawtooth, a stepped square, or sample-and-hold, which picks a new random value each cycle.

**Range.** Sine, triangle, sawtooth, square or sample-and-hold.

**Tip.** Sine is the natural choice for vibrato. A square makes the target jump between two values. Sample-and-hold gives a random, burbling pattern.
