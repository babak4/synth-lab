**What it does.** Sets how far the LFO pushes its destination.

**Range.** 0% to 100%. It starts at 0%, so the LFO does nothing until you raise it. At 100%, pitch moves two semitones either way.

**Tip.** Small amounts often sound best. Around 10% to 20% on pitch sounds like [vibrato](term:vibrato); much more starts to sound like a siren.
