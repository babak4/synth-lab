**What it does.** Shifts the computer keyboard's notes up or down by whole octaves, so the two rows of keys can reach the whole piano.

**Range.** −3 to +2 octaves, which keeps every key between C0 and C8.

**Tip.** Press the ← and → arrow keys to change it without leaving the keyboard.
