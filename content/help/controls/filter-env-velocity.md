**What it does.** Sets how much the [velocity](term:velocity) of your key press changes the filter envelope.

**Range.** 0% to 100%. It starts at 0%. At 0% every note is the same; at 100% the effect follows your velocity fully.

**Tip.** Change the keyboard velocity with the ↑ and ↓ keys and watch the dot on the envelope plot change height.
