**What it does.** Locks the echo time to the project's [tempo](term:tempo), so the repeats land on the beat.

**Range.** Off or on. When it is on, Division replaces Time.

**Tip.** Synced delay keeps its rhythm when you change the tempo, so you do not need to re-tune the time by ear.
