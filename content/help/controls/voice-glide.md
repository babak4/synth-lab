**What it does.** Sets how long the pitch takes to slide from one note to the next. It works in mono and legato modes.

**Range.** 0 (no glide) to 2 s. It starts at 0.

**Tip.** Around 50 to 150 ms gives a smooth, singing slide. At 0, notes jump straight to the new pitch.
