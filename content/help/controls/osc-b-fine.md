**What it does.** Nudges oscillator B's pitch by [cents](term:cent), hundredths of a semitone. That is too small to sound like a different note.

**Range.** −50 to +50 cents.

**Tip.** Detune the two oscillators against each other by a few cents and listen for slow [beating](term:beating) as they drift in and out of step.
