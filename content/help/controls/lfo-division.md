title: LFO division
kind: control
related: [lfo, lfo-sync]
presets: [wobble-bass]
---
**What it does.** When sync is on, sets how long one LFO cycle lasts, as a note length.

**Range.** 1/1 (a whole note, four beats) down to 1/32. At 120 BPM, 1/4 gives two cycles per second.

**Tip.** 1/8 gives a classic wobble. Slower divisions make long, evolving sweeps.
