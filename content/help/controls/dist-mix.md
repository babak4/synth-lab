title: Distortion mix
kind: control
related: [effects, dist-drive, dist-on]
presets: [wobble-bass, acid-squelch]
---
**What it does.** Sets the balance between the clean sound and the distorted one. Clean is called [dry](term:dry-wet); the effect's output is called wet.

**Range.** 0% (all clean) to 100% (all distorted).

**Tip.** Blend in about half for distortion that keeps the body of the original sound.
