**What it does.** Sets how long the volume takes to fall from its peak to the sustain level. This is the [decay](term:decay) stage.

**Range.** 1 ms to 5 s. It starts at 200 ms.

**Tip.** With sustain at 0%, the decay sets how long the note lasts. A short decay gives a pluck.
