**What it does.** Chooses what the LFO moves: pitch (vibrato), cutoff (wah), amp ([tremolo](term:tremolo)) or pulse width.

**Range.** One destination at a time.

**Tip.** If nothing seems to change, check the destination. Pulse width, for instance, only moves a pulse wave.
