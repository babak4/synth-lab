**What it does.** Sets the frequency where the filter starts to act. On a low-pass filter, sounds above the [cutoff](term:cutoff) get quieter.

**Range.** 20 Hz to 20 kHz. It starts at 8 kHz. Equal turns of the knob give equal musical steps, not equal steps in Hz.

**Tip.** Hold a sawtooth note and slowly turn the cutoff down. The sound loses its upper harmonics and gets darker.
