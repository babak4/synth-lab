title: Reverb mix
kind: control
related: [effects, reverb-on, reverb-size]
presets: [warm-pad, wind-sweep]
---
**What it does.** Sets how much of the reverberant sound is added to the original.

**Range.** 0% (none) to 100%. It starts at 25%.

**Tip.** More reverb makes a sound seem further away. For a sound that feels close, use less.
