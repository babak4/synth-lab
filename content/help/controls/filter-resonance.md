**What it does.** Boosts the sound right at the cutoff, giving the filter a ringing, vocal character. The boost is set with [Q](term:q).

**Range.** Q from 0.1 to 20. It starts at 1. High values whistle at the cutoff.

**Tip.** Turn it up, then move the cutoff slowly. The peak sweeps through the harmonics one by one. Keep the volume down, as resonance can get loud.
