**What it does.** Sets how loud the [noise](term:noise) source is. White noise is a hiss with the same energy at every frequency, with no pitch.

**Range.** 0% (silent) to 100%. It starts at 0%.

**Tip.** Send noise through a band-pass filter for wind and breath sounds. A touch of noise on a lead adds air.
