id: mix-osc-b
title: Oscillator B level
kind: control
related: [mixer, osc-b-waveform]
presets: [supersaw-lead]
---
**What it does.** Sets how loud oscillator B is in the mix that goes into the filter.

**Range.** 0% (silent) to 100%. It starts at 0%, so you need to raise it to hear oscillator B.

**Tip.** If you raise this and hear nothing, check that oscillator B's waveform is not set to Off.
