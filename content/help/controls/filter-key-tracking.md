id: filter-key-tracking
title: Filter key tracking
kind: control
related: [filter, filter-cutoff]
presets: [acid-squelch]
---
**What it does.** Makes the cutoff follow the key you play, so high notes open the filter more.

**Range.** 0%, 50% or 100%. At 100% the cutoff moves up one octave for each octave you play above middle C.

**Tip.** At 0%, high notes sound duller than low ones. Use 100% to keep the tone steady across the keyboard.
