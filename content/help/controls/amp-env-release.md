**What it does.** Sets how long the volume takes to fade after you let go of the key. This is the [release](term:release) stage.

**Range.** 1 ms to 10 s. It starts at 300 ms.

**Tip.** A long release lets notes overlap and blur together. A short one sounds tight and dry.
