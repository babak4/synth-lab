title: Distortion on
kind: control
related: [effects, dist-drive, dist-mix]
presets: [wobble-bass, acid-squelch]
---
**What it does.** Switches the distortion effect on or off.

**Range.** Off or on.

**Tip.** Distortion comes first in the effects chain, so the delay and reverb that follow it work on the distorted sound.
