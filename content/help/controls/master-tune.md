**What it does.** Tunes the whole synth by setting the pitch of A4, the A above middle C. Every other note moves with it.

**Range.** 430 to 450 Hz. The usual standard is 440 Hz.

**Tip.** Change it to play along with a recording or an instrument that is slightly out of tune with the standard.
