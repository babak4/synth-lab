**What it does.** When sync is on, sets the echo time as a note length.

**Range.** 1/1 (a whole note) down to 1/32. At 120 BPM, 1/8 is a quarter of a second.

**Tip.** A division of 1/8 gives a steady, driving echo. Try 1/4 for a slower, more spacious one.
