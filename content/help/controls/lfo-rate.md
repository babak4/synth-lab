**What it does.** Sets how fast the LFO cycles, in [hertz](term:hz): cycles per second.

**Range.** 0.05 Hz (one cycle every 20 seconds) to 20 Hz. It starts at 4 Hz.

**Tip.** Around 5 Hz sounds like natural vibrato. Above about 10 Hz the movement starts to blur into a buzz.
