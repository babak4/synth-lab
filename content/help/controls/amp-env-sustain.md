**What it does.** Sets the level the volume holds at for as long as you keep the key down. This is the [sustain](term:sustain) stage, and it is a level rather than a time.

**Range.** 0% to 100% of the peak. It starts at 80%.

**Tip.** At 0% the note dies away even while you hold the key, like a plucked string or a drum.
