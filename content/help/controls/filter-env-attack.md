**What it does.** Sets how long the filter envelope takes to rise to its peak after you press a key. This is the [attack](term:attack) stage.

**Range.** 1 ms to 5 s. It starts at 5 ms.

**Tip.** A long attack makes each note start dark and slowly brighten, like a swell.
