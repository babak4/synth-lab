**What it does.** Sets how hard the notes you play are struck. A computer keyboard cannot sense force, so you choose it here.

**Range.** 1 to 127. The ↑ and ↓ keys change it in steps of 16.

**Tip.** If every note sounds the same however you set this, check the velocity controls on the envelopes. They decide how much velocity matters.
