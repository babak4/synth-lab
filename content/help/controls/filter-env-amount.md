title: Filter envelope amount

**What it does.** Sets how far the filter [envelope](term:envelope) moves the cutoff on every note. Positive values open the filter; negative values close it.

**Range.** −100% to +100%. At 100% the cutoff can sweep up by six octaves. It starts at 0%, so the envelope does nothing at first.

**Tip.** Set the cutoff low, the amount high and the filter decay short for a plucky bass.
