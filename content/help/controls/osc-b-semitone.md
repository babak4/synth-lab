**What it does.** Moves oscillator B up or down in [semitones](term:semitone), the steps on a piano keyboard.

**Range.** −12 to +12 semitones, which is one octave either way.

**Tip.** Set oscillator B seven semitones above A and every key plays a fifth: an instant power chord.
