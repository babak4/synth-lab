**What it does.** Sets how long the filter envelope takes to fall from its peak to the sustain level. This is the [decay](term:decay) stage.

**Range.** 1 ms to 5 s. It starts at 300 ms.

**Tip.** Pair a short decay with a high envelope amount on the filter for a quick, plucky "zap".
