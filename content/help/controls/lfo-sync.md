title: LFO tempo sync
kind: control
related: [lfo, lfo-division, lfo-rate]
presets: [wobble-bass]
---
**What it does.** Locks the LFO's speed to the project's [tempo](term:tempo), so it moves in time with the music.

**Range.** Off or on. When it is on, Division replaces Rate.

**Tip.** A synced wobble stays in time even when you change the tempo.
