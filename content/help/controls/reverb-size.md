title: Reverb size
kind: control
related: [effects, reverb-mix]
presets: [warm-pad, wind-sweep]
---
**What it does.** Chooses the size of the space. Short is a small, tight room. Room is a typical room. Hall is a large space with a long tail.

**Range.** Short (a tail of about half a second), room (about 1.3 seconds) or hall (about 3 seconds).

**Tip.** Big spaces blur fast notes together. Use hall on slow pads and short on busy parts.
