**What it does.** Sets the overall volume of everything you hear. A [limiter](term:limiter) after it protects your ears and speakers from sudden peaks.

**Range.** −60 dB (silent) to 0 dB. It starts at −12 dB. Every 6 dB is roughly a doubling of the signal.

**Tip.** Keep it low while you try new sounds. Resonance and distortion can suddenly get much louder.
