**What it does.** Sets the level the filter envelope holds at for as long as you keep the key down. This is the [sustain](term:sustain) stage, and it is a level rather than a time.

**Range.** 0% to 100% of the peak. It starts at 30%.

**Tip.** At 0% the filter closes completely after the decay, so the held part of the note is dark.
