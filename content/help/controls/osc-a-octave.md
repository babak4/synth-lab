**What it does.** Shifts oscillator A up or down in whole octaves. Each [octave](term:octave) doubles or halves the [frequency](term:frequency).

**Range.** −2 to +2 octaves. 0 plays the pitch of the key you press.

**Tip.** Put one oscillator an octave above the other and turn its level down a little for a fuller, organ-like sound.
