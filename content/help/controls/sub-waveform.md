**What it does.** Adds a sub-oscillator that plays below oscillator A and follows its pitch. Choose a sine for a smooth low rumble or a square for a harder edge.

**Range.** Off, sine or square.

**Tip.** Turn up the Sub level in the mixer to hear it. It adds weight without making the sound brighter.
