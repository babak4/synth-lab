---
id: lfo-retrigger
title: LFO retrigger
kind: control
related: [lfo, lfo-rate]
presets: [wobble-bass]
---
**What it does.** When on, the LFO restarts at the beginning of its cycle with every note. When off, it runs freely and each note catches it at a different point.

**Range.** Off or on.

**Tip.** Turn it on for movement that should start the same way every time, such as a wobble that begins on the beat.
